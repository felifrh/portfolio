Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA, part of Microsoft). When you open a page, GitHub's servers receive technical data that your browser sends anyway, such as your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested and the browser type, and store it in server log files. I have no access to these logs. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in providing the website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub states that it transfers personal data from the EEA to the USA on the basis of the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Without this processing the website cannot be delivered. GitHub's own privacy statement explains how long it keeps its logs.
