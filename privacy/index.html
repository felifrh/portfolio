Privacy

In short

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking, contact forms, external fonts, external scripts or embedded third-party content. Fonts, images and videos are hosted on the same server as the pages.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA, part of Microsoft). When you open a page, GitHub's servers receive technical data that your browser sends anyway, such as your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested and the browser type, and store it in server log files. I have no access to these logs. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in providing the website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub may process data in the USA; see GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Domain

The domain feliferch.com is registered with Checkdomain. Checkdomain handles the domain name system; visitors' browsers may query it to find this website.

Data in your browser

While you move between pages, your browser keeps a short note in its session storage so that the transition from the home page to a project can play smoothly. It contains no personal data and disappears when you close the tab. If you use a phone, the website may read the device's motion sensor to tilt images. This stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Email

If you write to me, I process your address and message to answer you. I delete them when the conversation is finished, unless I am required to keep them. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg (LfDI Baden-Württemberg).
