DDW something.beyond

The visual identity and a smartphone website for Pforzheim University's exhibition at Dutch Design Week 2026.

Year
2026
Context
exhibition "something.beyond", Faculty of Design, Pforzheim University, Dutch Design Week
Team
with Katharina Wittemann
Role
developed the identity together; focus on the digital part (website, NFC, animation, reel)
Tools
corporate identity, print, web design, animation, NFC programming

01 / Identity

For the Faculty of Design's exhibition at Dutch Design Week 2026 we developed a corporate identity for print and digital. It is built from dots modelled on the perforated stones used in the exhibition. Print consists of take-away postcards describing each project and a poster; digital of an animation, an Instagram reel and a website.

02 / Website

The title "something.beyond" becomes tangible through NFC chips placed at every exhibit. One scan with a phone leads directly to the respective project with further images and texts. The website is designed entirely for smartphones, with light and dark mode, and gives an overview of all projects at a glance. The concept was developed without AI; because of the short time, AI was used only to refine the website.
